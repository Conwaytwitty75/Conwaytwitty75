🛑 STOP SIGN WIRING SCHEMATIC

12V System with Triton EL13 Heavy Duty Flasher

Components List:

  • Power Supply: 12V DC
  • Rocker Switch: 3-Prong (Power, Load, Ground)
  • Flasher Unit: Triton EL13 Heavy Duty Flasher
  • Bulbs: Two 12V bulbs
  • Housing Unit: Contains switch and flasher
WIRING COLOR CODE:
  • RED: +12V Power from supply
  • BLUE: Switched +12V (through rocker switch)
  • ORANGE: Flasher outputs to bulbs
  • BLACK: Ground/Negative
WIRING INSTRUCTIONS:
  1. Power Supply Connection:
    • Connect +12V (RED) from power supply to Power terminal on rocker switch
    • Connect Ground (BLACK) from power supply to Ground terminal on rocker switch
  2. Rocker Switch to Flasher:
    • Connect Load terminal from rocker switch to Input on Triton EL13 flasher
    • Ground from switch connects to common ground bus
  3. Flasher to Bulbs:
    • Connect Triton EL13 Output 1 to positive terminal of Bulb 1
    • Connect Triton EL13 Output 2 to positive terminal of Bulb 2
    • Connect negative terminals of both bulbs to common ground bus
  4. Housing Unit:
    • Mount rocker switch and Triton EL13 flasher inside housing unit
    • Ensure all connections are secure and insulated
    • Use appropriate wire gauge for 12V automotive/marine applications (recommend 16-18 AWG)
⚠️ SAFETY NOTES:
  • Always disconnect power before wiring
  • Ensure all connections are properly insulated
  • Verify polarity before connecting bulbs
  • Use appropriate fuse protection (recommend 5-10A fuse at power supply)
  • Ensure housing unit is weatherproof if used outdoors
  • Check all connections for tightness before applying power
TRITON EL13 SPECIFICATIONS:
  • Heavy duty electronic flasher unit
  • 12V DC operation
  • Typically 60-90 flashes per minute
  • Can handle multiple bulbs per output
  • Two independent outputs for alternating flash pattern
